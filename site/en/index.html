Self-Hosted · Local SQLite Persistence · Zero Telemetry SDKs

Self-Hosted Management Panel & Observability Dashboard for CPA / CLIProxyAPI

Operate providers, credentials, OAuth, plugins, and configuration while keeping persistent request history, cost analytics, quota windows, failure diagnostics, and account automation in local storage.

CPAMP manages and observes traffic through CPA / CLIProxyAPI — it is not a replacement proxy and does not forward model traffic by itself. No account registration required; all state stays in your local files.

What Can CPAMP Help You Answer?

Unify day-to-day gateway administration and long-horizon observability in one self-hosted workspace.

Why are requests failing?

Inspect failure rates, HTTP status codes, latency, traces, affected models or accounts, and redacted failure summaries in persistent local SQLite history without exposing raw failure bodies.

Where is the cost going?
